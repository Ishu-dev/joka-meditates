Every case, every cold call, every mid-term here is built to sharpen one thing. It matters. It just isn't the only thing that matters.

Control the mind.
Don't let the mind control you.

The other two intelligences don't show up on a transcript. They show up in the interview, the rejection, the 2am spiral before a submission. This issue is about training for those too.

Framework

The Three Quotients

IQ

Logic, pattern, recall. The kind of intelligence a classroom is built to measure.

EQ

Reading and regulating emotion, yours and the room's. It's the difference between a case interview that goes well and one that doesn't, and it's usually the real cause when a team falls apart, not the deadline. One way to build it: name what you're feeling before you react to it. That half-second gap is most of the skill.

SQ

The least familiar of the three. Not religion. The capacity to sit with uncertainty without needing to resolve it immediately, and to know what you're actually optimizing for beyond the next number on a stipend letter. Hardest to explain, most useful under pressure.

Input

Your Feed Is Training Data

You audit your diet this month already. Audit what you follow too. What shows up in your feed daily becomes the default shape of your thoughts, whether you notice it happening or not.

Unfollow whatever leaves you comparing or resentful, even if it's entertaining in the moment. Follow what leaves you calmer or sharper. Put good out where you can, it tends to find its way back, not as karma bookkeeping, just as how people and rooms actually work.

Body

Move, Even for Ten Minutes

Start small. Ten minutes, a short walk, a stretch, anything. Don't wait for the ideal routine before beginning, the bar for day one should be embarrassingly low.

You'll regret every week you didn't start, once you're a few weeks into actually doing it. That's the whole pitch. Start, then build from there.

Practice

Nadi Shodhana

Alternate nostril breathing, the classical technique for balancing exactly the split this issue is about, one side active, one side still.

Right thumb closes the right nostril, breathe in left. Close left with the ring finger, release right, breathe out right. In right, close right, breathe out left. That's one round.

Min 5, max 50
